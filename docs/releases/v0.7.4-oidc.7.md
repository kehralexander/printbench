# PrintBench v0.7.4-oidc.7

Fork build of PrintBench 0.7.4 (OIDC sign-in, adding files to models, G-code
previews, print-queue details, Spoolman inventory, printer status and job
tracking) that works through the follow-up list from the job tracking release.

## Highlights

- **Printers on the dashboard.** A card per printer: idle or busy, and when
  busy the file, percentage and "done around" time, linking to the status page.
  Read from the worker's last snapshot, so a printer that is off never slows
  the dashboard down.
- **What to print next.** When a printer is idle, the queue, the status page and
  the request itself say what it could start with the filament it has loaded
  ("Ready to start: Benchy for Sam (slot 2)"). When the most urgent request
  needs different filament, they say what to load ("Most urgent is Red clip:
  load spool #21 · PETG").
- **Spool burn-down.** With Spoolman, the queue adds up the grams of every open
  request per spool, in queue order and counting only what the running print
  still needs. It warns when a spool will not last for everything queued on it:
  "Spool #17 runs out after 2 of the 3 queued prints on it". The requests past
  that point say so too.
- **Drag to reorder the queue.** Drag a request by its handle, or use the arrow
  keys on the handle, to give the open queue a manual order. That order also
  decides which request the tracker picks when a printer starts a file several
  requests are waiting for. "Reset order" goes back to priority, due date and
  age.
- **Status links for requesters.** Every request has a status link and QR code
  that needs no account. It shows that one request: its place in the queue and
  a likely ready time, or the live progress and "ready around" while it prints,
  or that it is ready to collect. It updates by itself from the tracker.
- **How did it go?** Prints the printer logged are listed as unrated on the
  dashboard and the print history for two weeks. One click on a star rates
  one. A note, a photo, or "it failed after all" are optional, and Skip stops
  the prompt for that print. Photos are kept under `DATA_DIR/photos` and shown
  in the print history.
- **Printer jobs not in the library.** Jobs whose file PrintBench did not have
  (started from the printer's screen, or sent straight from a slicer) are
  listed under Printer status → "N prints not in the library". From there you
  can:
  - link a job to a model, which writes the print history entry after the
    fact, for every print of that file;
  - fetch the file from the printer (Moonraker/KX-Bridge, OctoPrint) into a
    model's folder;
  - dismiss it.

  Jobs whose file turns up in the library later are linked by the worker on
  its own.

- **Success rates.** Print history → Success rates shows how often prints
  succeed by model, file and material. For failed prints it also shows where
  they usually stop, from the printer's own progress, with a hint (for example,
  stopping in the first layers suggests bed adhesion).
- **OIDC auto-provisioning.** With `OIDC_ALLOW_SIGNUP=true`, accounts are
  created on the first SSO sign-in, with a role from the provider's groups
  claim:
  - `OIDC_ADMIN_GROUPS` and `OIDC_MEMBER_GROUPS` map groups to roles; anyone
    else is a viewer.
  - `OIDC_ALLOWED_GROUPS` refuses anyone in none of the listed groups, with a
    clear message on the login page.
  - `OIDC_GROUPS_CLAIM` names the claim, `groups` by default.

  The role is re-applied on every SSO sign-in for accounts that only sign in
  through SSO. Accounts with a password keep the role set here. New accounts
  take their name and e-mail from the provider, and the e-mail counts as
  verified. Provisioning, role changes and refusals are recorded in the audit
  log.

## Upgrade notes

- One migration (`0021_queue_followups`) adds `position` and `share_token` to
  `print_requests`, `dismissed_at` to `printer_jobs` and `review_dismissed_at`
  to `print_runs`. It is dated directly after the previous fork migration.
- Status links (`/r/<token>`) and their QR codes (`/r/<token>/qr`) are served
  without a session. They show one request's title, requester, material and
  progress, nothing else. The QR code encodes `BETTER_AUTH_URL` (or `APP_URL`)
  when set.
- Print photos are stored under `DATA_DIR/photos`. Keep that directory on the
  persistent volume, like the previews.
- OIDC: nothing changes unless the new variables are set. Without
  `OIDC_ADMIN_GROUPS` or `OIDC_MEMBER_GROUPS`, roles are not touched by
  sign-in, and new SSO accounts are viewers as before. The provider must put
  the groups in the ID token or userinfo. Authentik does this with its default
  `profile` scope.
- New web dependency: `qrcode`.
