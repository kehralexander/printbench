# PrintBench v0.7.4-oidc.10

Fork build of PrintBench 0.7.4 (OIDC sign-in, adding files to models, G-code
previews, print-queue details, Spoolman inventory, printer status, job tracking,
the follow-up list, the merged Models page and the KX-Bridge printer type) that
stops logging a printer's warm-up as cancelled prints.

## Highlights

- **No more cancels at 0% before a print.** The Kobra X behind KX-Bridge reads
  idle for moments while it heats, levels and calibrates. Each of those used to
  end the job, so one print showed up as several "Cancelled at 0%" failures
  followed by the real one. A printer that goes quiet without saying how the
  job ended now keeps its job open for three minutes. If it starts printing
  again in that time, it is the same print.
- A printer that does go quiet for good is logged as cancelled from the moment
  it stopped, not three minutes later. One that says complete, cancelled or
  failed is logged straight away, as before.
- A printer reporting busy without a file name, between preparation steps, no
  longer ends its job either.

## Upgrade notes

- One migration, `0023_printer_job_idle`, adds a column to `printer_jobs`. It is
  safe on a running database.
- A cancel that KX-Bridge reports as plain idle now reaches the history and the
  queue up to three minutes later.
- Entries already logged during warm-ups stay in the history; delete them from
  the model's print history if you want them gone.
